Executive summary

A dependable, local egg supply — ready now.

Thank you for inviting MKG Egg Farm to submit a proposal. We are a family-run layer farm in the highlands of Mokhotlong, producing 5 500 fresh eggs every day, hand-graded into Large, Medium and Small. We propose to become a regular supplier of fresh table eggs to Shoprite, delivered on a fixed weekly schedule, graded to consistent weight bands and packed to your requirements — giving your stores a reliable, genuinely local product that reaches the shelf days fresher than eggs trucked in over long distances.

We are proposing a straightforward start: a four-week pilot into the stores or distribution point of your choice, followed by a standing supply agreement once quality, fill-rate and delivery performance have been proven against your standards.

01 · The product

Three grades, one standard.

Every egg is graded by weight into the size class below, inspected for cracks, dirt and shell defects, and only first-grade eggs are packed for retail. Weight bands follow the standard retail size classes used across Southern Africa.

GradeWeight per eggTypical use on shelfPack
Large51 g – 59 gEveryday family staple — the core of the egg fixtureTray of 30
Medium43 g – 51 gValue tier and bakingTray of 30
Small33 g – 43 gEntry price point; bakery and in-store kitchenTray of 30

Total output is 5 500 eggs per day across all three grades. The size mix moves naturally with flock age, so we share a weekly grading forecast and agree the allocation by grade with your buyer before each order cycle. Retail cartons of 6, 12 or 18 can be supplied on request.

02 · Quality & food safety

  • Collected daily, several times a day, to protect shell quality and freshness.
  • Hand-graded and inspected — cracked, dirty or misshapen eggs never leave the farm.
  • Balanced nutrition from iTau Complete Lay feed, for consistent shell strength and yolk colour.
  • Cool, clean storage on the farm and covered, clean transport to delivery.
  • Pack date and best-before date on every tray, for full stock rotation (FIFO).
  • Open farm — Shoprite's quality team is welcome to audit our site at any time.

03 · Supply & logistics

From nest to shelf in a fixed rhythm.

1OrderShoprite places weekly order by grade
2CollectEggs gathered daily in Mokhotlong
3Grade & packWeighed, inspected, dated, packed
4DeliverScheduled delivery to DC or stores
5ReviewWeekly fill-rate and quality check-in

Proposed schedule: two to three deliveries per week to Shoprite's nominated distribution centre or stores in Lesotho, on fixed days agreed with your logistics team. Weekly orders confirmed by an agreed cut-off; eggs delivered within 72 hours of grading. Delivery note and invoice with every drop; any short-supply flagged to your buyer before dispatch, never on arrival.

05 · Proposed terms

Volume
Up to 5 500 eggs a day (≈183 trays) reserved for Shoprite; grade mix agreed weekly.
Pilot
Four-week pilot at agreed volumes and stores, then a 12-month supply agreement.
Price review
Fixed for the pilot and first three months; then reviewed quarterly against feed costs, on 30 days' notice.
Payment
30 days from statement, by EFT, or in line with Shoprite's standard supplier terms.
Quality claims
Any tray not meeting grade or arriving damaged is credited or replaced on the next delivery.
Compliance
Company registration, trading licence, tax clearance and health certificate supplied at onboarding.

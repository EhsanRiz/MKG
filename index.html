Our Story

Raised where the air is clear.

MKG Egg Farm began with a simple conviction: that a good egg starts with a well-kept hen and honest, unhurried work. In the mountains above Mokhotlong — one of the highest inhabited places on the continent — our flock lives at a pace the highlands set. Cool days, clean water, room to move.

We are a family farm, and we treat every crate the way we would one bound for our own table. There are no shortcuts here, only a steady rhythm: feed at first light, collect through the day, grade by hand, and deliver while the eggs are at their freshest. It is quiet work, and we are proud of it.

“An egg is only as good as the day it left the farm.”

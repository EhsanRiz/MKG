Our Story

Raised where the air is clear.

MKG Egg Farm began with a simple conviction: that a good egg starts with a well-kept hen and honest, unhurried work. In the mountains above Mokhotlong — one of the highest inhabited places on the continent — our flock has been laying since 2018. Cool days, clean water, steady and careful hands.

We are a family farm, and we treat every crate the way we would one bound for our own table. There are no shortcuts here, only a steady rhythm: feed at first light, collect through the day, grade by hand, and deliver while the eggs are at their freshest. It is quiet work, and we are proud of it.

“An egg is only as good as the day it left the farm.”

FEPA Highlands · Feed Enhancement & Productivity for Agriculture

We feed the highlands, too.

In Sesotho, fepa means to feed. FEPA Highlands is our egg and feed programme for highland farmers, run from the MKG farm at Mokhotlong with 4D Climate Solutions and supplied at factory price by iTau Animal Feeds.

Supply

Scientifically formulated iTau feed in 50 kg bags — layer, broiler and pig feed stocked at our depot, with the wider iTau range to order — so highland farmers pay a fair price.

Train

Farmer workshops on animal nutrition and feed management, with species-specific feeding guides in Sesotho and English and a lead-farmer network for peer support.

Track — FeedTrack

Every farmer gets a FeedTrack feed book: a QR-linked record of purchases and daily feeding. Lose the book and the depot reprints it free — the record is safe in the system, built with 4D Climate Solutions.
